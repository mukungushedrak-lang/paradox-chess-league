How to participate

  1. Create your own Lichess account.
  2. Open the tournament you want to play.
  3. Read its rules and check whether joining is open.
  4. Join on Lichess using your account.
  5. Follow pairings and results on Lichess.

Registration, pairings and results are handled on Lichess. This preview does not collect player data or payments.

Fair play

All participants must follow Lichess fair-play rules and the conditions published by the organiser.
